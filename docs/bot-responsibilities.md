# Bot Responsibilities

## Primary Responsibilities

The Raven should eventually support five major responsibility areas.

## 1. Guide

Help players understand the server:

- How to join.
- Server rules.
- Roleplay expectations.
- House structure.
- Progression rules.
- Rebellion mechanics.
- Boss and biome pacing.

## 2. Chronicler

Preserve the history of the realm:

- Major construction milestones.
- Boss kills.
- House founding and collapse.
- Wars.
- Alliances.
- Betrayals.
- Kingly decrees.
- Important administrative decisions.
- Roleplay events.

## 3. Administrator Assistant

Support the King and staff:

- Draft announcements.
- Summarize known events.
- Prepare reminders.
- Retrieve rulings.
- Organize realm facts.
- Assist with onboarding material.

## 4. Storyteller

Add restrained flavor:

- Scheduled lore messages.
- Event recaps.
- Achievement celebrations.
- Seasonal summaries.
- House chronicles.

The storyteller mode should be used deliberately. The Raven should not interrupt normal chat or turn every response into performance.

## 5. Future Server Intelligence Layer

When GameOps Bridge is available, the Raven may consume live information:

- Player joins and leaves.
- Boss kills.
- Deaths.
- Biome discoveries.
- Structure events.
- Server uptime.
- Raid activity.
- Administrative actions.

This responsibility must remain loosely coupled and event-driven.

## Non-Responsibilities

- The bot should not enforce every rule automatically at launch.
- The bot should not pretend uncertain information is true.
- The bot should not spam channels with lore.
- The bot should not replace human moderation judgment.
