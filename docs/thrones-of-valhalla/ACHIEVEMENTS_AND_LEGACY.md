# Achievements And Legacy

## Purpose

Achievements, titles, and Chronicle entries give the Realm memory. They help players see what mattered, who shaped history, and which moments became permanent.

The system should feel like Xbox-style achievements layered over a living Valheim kingdom: permanent bragging rights for meaningful accomplishments, not daily chores.

## Achievements, Titles, And Chronicle Entries

### Achievements

Achievements are permanent badges.

They should be:

- Permanent.
- Objective or semi-objective.
- Easy to understand.
- Lightly trackable.
- Meaningful as bragging rights.
- Safe to display in a read-only achievement feed.

Achievements should not be removed because politics change. If a player earned a badge, the Realm remembers it.

### Titles

Titles are social recognition.

They may change over time as politics, tournaments, leadership, and Crown favor change. A player may lose a title, gain a better title, or hold a title that becomes historically ironic.

Example titles:

- Champion of the Realm.
- Warden of the North.
- Master Builder.
- Master of Coin.
- Kingslayer.
- Defender of the Realm.
- Hand of the King.

Titles should feel public and social. They do not need to be permanent achievements.

### Chronicle Entries

Chronicle entries are permanent story records for major events.

The Chronicle should capture:

- Boss defeats.
- House founding or collapse.
- Major alliances.
- Major betrayals.
- Major wars.
- Royal Hunts.
- Crown decrees.
- Succession events.
- Landmark builds.
- Tournament results when important.

The Chronicle is not a feed for routine play.

## Achievement Principles

Good achievements:

- Mark memorable participation.
- Encourage players to join meaningful events.
- Recognize different play styles.
- Can be tracked automatically, by one-click confirmation, or by rare staff confirmation.
- Do not require daily participation.
- Do not require exact item accounting.
- Do not create pressure to grind boring tasks.

Bad achievements:

- Log in every day for 30 days.
- Donate exact resource counts repeatedly.
- Submit weekly screenshots.
- Kill a large number of routine enemies.
- Require staff to verify ordinary activity.
- Reward chores more than stories.

## Tracking Requirements

Achievement requirements should stay light and trackable.

Preferred tracking:

- Automatic where possible.
- One-click confirmation where reasonable.
- Short modal only for meaningful context.
- Admin-confirmed for major moments.

Avoid:

- Screenshots as routine proof.
- Daily reports.
- Manual spreadsheets.
- Exact per-player resource ledgers.
- Hidden criteria that players cannot understand.

## Example Achievement Categories

### Founder Achievements

Founder achievements recognize early and structural contributions.

Examples:

- First sworn members of the Realm.
- Founding member of a recognized House.
- Founder of a cadet branch.
- Witness to the first Crown decree.
- Builder of an early public landmark.

### House Achievements

House achievements recognize group identity and House-level progress.

Examples:

- House recognized by the Crown.
- House completed its first settlement.
- House supported a Royal Tribute.
- House fielded fighters in a Royal Hunt.
- House formed a recognized alliance.
- House survived a major war.

House achievements should not require perfect member-by-member contribution tracking.

### Royal Hunt Achievements

Royal Hunt achievements recognize participation in boss milestones.

Examples:

- Supported the First Royal Hunt.
- Fought in the Hunt for Eikthyr.
- Stood at the fall of the Elder.
- Survived a Royal Hunt.
- Crown representative at an altar opening.
- House present for a Realm boss defeat.

Participation can be confirmed broadly. The system should not require exact damage logs or perfect attendance records.

### Tournament Achievements

Tournament achievements recognize competition.

Examples:

- Won a Crown tournament.
- Reached a tournament final.
- Defended a champion title.
- Won a House-versus-House contest.
- Claimed Champion of the Realm.

Tournament results are strong achievement candidates because they are discrete and easy to confirm.

### Builder Achievements

Builder achievements recognize construction that becomes part of the Realm's identity.

Examples:

- Built a recognized House hall.
- Contributed to King's Landing.
- Built a landmark bridge, harbor, road, arena, or fort.
- Created a settlement recognized by the Crown.
- Restored or expanded a public structure.

Builder achievements should focus on recognized builds, not every routine building change.

### Explorer Achievements

Explorer achievements recognize discovery and travel.

Examples:

- Charted a major route.
- Discovered a future settlement site.
- Reached a distant biome for the Realm.
- Led a voyage that mattered.
- Found a location later used for a Royal Hunt or settlement.

Explorer achievements should be based on meaningful discoveries, not exhaustive map tracking.

### Helper And Community Achievements

Helper achievements recognize players who make the server better.

Examples:

- Helped onboard new players.
- Supported a House outside their own.
- Provided gear or food for a Royal Hunt.
- Helped recover from a disaster.
- Served as a trusted organizer.

These may require House leader, Crown, or moderator confirmation. They should not become popularity contests or daily labor awards.

### Year One And Legacy Achievements

Legacy achievements preserve the early history of the Realm.

Examples:

- Year One participant.
- Witness to the First Royal Hunt.
- Member of a founding House.
- Survived the first succession crisis.
- Helped build the first King's Landing.
- Present for the final boss before the Age of Succession.

Legacy achievements are especially important because the Realm has no planned wipes.

## Titles

Titles recognize current status, public reputation, political office, or notable identity.

Examples:

- Champion of the Realm.
- Warden of the North.
- Master Builder.
- Master of Coin.
- Kingslayer.
- Defender of the Realm.
- Hand of the King.
- Lord Commander.
- Royal Huntsman.
- First Builder of King's Landing.

Titles may be:

- Granted by the Crown.
- Won in a tournament.
- Earned through service.
- Claimed through succession.
- Lost through defeat, betrayal, decree, or changing politics.

A title can be historically important without being permanent.

## Chronicle

The Chronicle is the permanent story log.

It should record major events only:

- Boss defeats.
- Royal Hunt results.
- Major tournament outcomes.
- House founding, splitting, or collapse.
- Landmark builds.
- Crown decrees.
- Major wars.
- Betrayals that change the Realm.
- Succession events.

The Chronicle should not record every achievement, every title change, or every routine play session.

## Read-Only Feeds

The Realm should eventually support:

- A read-only Achievement feed.
- A read-only Chronicle feed.
- Public summaries of current titles when useful.

Read-only feeds keep recognition visible without creating noisy back-and-forth channels.

## Permanent Bragging Rights

Achievements are not chores. They are permanent proof that a player or House was part of something worth remembering.

The best achievement test is:

- Would a player be proud to show this later?
- Can the Raven track it without creating paperwork?
- Does it tell a story about the Realm?

If the answer is no, it probably should not be an achievement.
